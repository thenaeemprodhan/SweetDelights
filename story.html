Our story

Three generations of sweetness

From a single family kitchen to a much-loved neighbourhood shop, this is the story of Sweet Delights.

“A sweet made in a hurry never tastes quite right.”

That was the first thing our grandmother taught us, and it is still how we work. Sweet Delights began in a small family kitchen, where festivals meant trays of ladoo cooling on every surface and neighbours knocking at the door.

Today we make sweets from three traditions: rich Indian classics, delicate Bengali chhena sweets and fragrant Turkish delights. The ingredients are simple and the methods are the old ones, and every piece is made by hand.

Our journey

Made the same way for over 25 years

A lot has changed since 1998, but the way we make our sweets has not.

  1. 1998

    A family kitchen

    Our founder begins making her mother's recipes for friends and neighbours in East London.

  2. 2006

    The first shop

    A small shopfront opens on Market Street, with jalebi fried in the window.

  3. 2015

    A new tradition

    Turkish lokum and baklava join the counter, inspired by our neighbours and friends.

  4. Today

    Three generations

    The same recipes, now made by the next generation, still by hand and still fresh every morning.

Handcrafted daily

Every sweet is shaped by hand in small batches each morning, never mass-produced.

Natural ingredients

Pure ghee, fresh milk, real saffron and nuts, with no artificial colours or preservatives.

Family recipes

Recipes passed down through three generations and still made the slow, traditional way.
